# ASVS 5.0 mapping: authentication, session management, authorization (Stage 1)

Target: Level 2 overall, Level 3 for authentication and session management (docs/kit/08). Chapters V1-V5 and V9-V17 are mapped when the stages that introduce their surface land; V6, V7 and V8 are the Stage 1 sign-off set.
